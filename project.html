Fraud Insights Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard analyzing 10K+ Indian consumer transactions to uncover patterns in fraud, spending behavior, and regional risk.

Overview

This dashboard focuses on identifying fraud patterns across 10,000+ consumer transactions in India. It combines data cleaning in Python with Power BI visualization to explore when, where, and how fraudulent activity occurs.

  • Tools Used: Python (pandas, numpy), Power BI, DAX, GitHub
  • Goal: Detect fraud trends, understand transaction behavior, and visualize high-risk segments.

Key Metrics

  • Total Transactions: 10,270
  • Fraudulent Transactions: 5,046
  • Fraud Rate: 52.44%
  • Average Fraud Amount: ₹5,097.90
  • Average Revenue per Customer: ₹48,292.45

Insights

  • Fraudulent transactions make up over half of all transactions analyzed.
  • Online Shopping and Entertainment categories show the highest fraud rates.
  • Fraud values peak in the ₹10K–₹30K range, with smaller transactions being the most frequent.
  • Fraud spikes during October–December, aligning with major festive sale periods.
  • Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu record the highest average fraud amounts.

Outcome

The Fraud Insights Dashboard presents a comprehensive view of fraudulent behavior across different dimensions—time, category, amount, and geography. It enables business and risk teams to quickly identify high-risk areas, quantify financial impact, and prioritize preventive measures.
